⚠ Important — Please Read

TotalStationTech provides field construction layout coaching and consulting only. This is not land surveying, and TotalStationTech is not a licensed land surveying firm.

Construction layout — placing points from an approved plan for building purposes — does not require a survey license in most jurisdictions. Boundary surveys, legal descriptions, and land surveying services must be performed by a licensed land surveyor in your state.

If your project requires boundary determination, ALTA surveys, or any service requiring a licensed surveyor, please engage a licensed land surveying firm directly. Nothing on this site should be interpreted as land surveying services or advice.
